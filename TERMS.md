# Terms of Use

**Last updated:** 27 July 2026

These are the terms you accept when you start using Fichess. The same text is
shown inside the app before you log in for the first time.

Fichess is an independent, unofficial client for the Free Internet Chess Server
(FICS, [freechess.org](https://www.freechess.org)). Games, accounts and chat live
on FICS, which is operated by its own administrators.

## Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour

The app lets you exchange messages with other players. **There is no tolerance
for objectionable content or abusive users.** You must not send, request or
encourage messages that are offensive, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually
explicit, or that target anyone because of who they are. You are responsible for
what you send.

Messages you receive are written by other people on FICS, not by the developer of
this app.

## What you can do

**Filtering.** Offensive language is filtered automatically. Messages caught by
the filter are hidden behind a warning; you can reveal them if you choose to.

**Reporting.** You can report any message you consider objectionable. Press and
hold the message and choose *Report message*. The report reaches the developer
directly.

**Blocking.** You can block any player. Press and hold one of their messages and
choose *Block player*, or manage the list from Settings. Blocking takes effect
immediately: their messages disappear from the app at once, and the block is also
applied on the FICS server, so they can no longer reach you there either. You can
unblock at any time from Settings.

## What we do

Reports are **reviewed within 24 hours**. Where a report is upheld:

- the offending content is removed from the app,
- the player is blocked,
- the account is reported to the FICS administrators, who are responsible for
  sanctioning accounts on their server.

Accounts that repeatedly send objectionable content lose access to the messaging
features of this app.

Because accounts live on FICS and not in this app, the decision to suspend or
remove a FICS account rests with the FICS administrators. What this app can
always do — immediately and without waiting for anyone — is stop the content
reaching you.

## Using the app

Fichess is provided free of charge, as is, without warranty. It depends on a
third-party server that the developer does not operate: availability, ratings,
game results and account policies are FICS's, not ours.

You need a FICS account, or you can play as a guest. Registration is free at
[freechess.org](https://www.freechess.org).
