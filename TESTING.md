# Become a tester

Fichess is in closed testing on Google Play before its public release. Testers
get the app for free, ahead of everyone else, and their feedback shapes what
ships.

Android only for now. iOS testing goes through TestFlight — write to the address
below if you would like to take part on iPhone.

## How to join

**1. Send me your Google account address**

Email **casali.thomas@gmail.com** with the subject "Fichess tester" and tell me
the Gmail address associated with your Android phone. It has to be the account
you actually use on the device — Google Play matches testers by that account,
and any other address will not work.

Your address is used only to add you to the tester list on Google Play. It is not
published, shared or used for anything else, and you can ask to be removed at any
time.

**2. Wait for confirmation**

I add you to the list manually, so give it a day or so. I will reply when you are
in.

**3. Accept the invitation**

Once you are on the list, open this link on your phone, signed in with that same
Google account, and accept:

**https://play.google.com/apps/testing/work.ficschess.app**

> If that page shows an error, it means one of two things: you are not on the
> tester list yet, or the test has not opened yet. Both resolve themselves —
> just wait for my confirmation email.

**4. Install from Google Play**

After accepting, the Play Store page for Fichess becomes available to you and you
can install it normally. Updates arrive automatically like any other app.

## What I ask in return

Nothing formal — play a few games and tell me what felt wrong.

There is one thing that genuinely matters, though: **please stay opted in for at
least two weeks.** Google requires a closed test to run with a minimum number of
testers continuously enrolled before the app can be released publicly. If people
opt in and then leave after a couple of days, the clock resets and the public
release is delayed for everyone.

If you find a bug, [open an issue](https://github.com/thomascasali/fichess/issues/new/choose)
or just reply to my email. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for what makes a report
useful.

## What you need

- An Android phone with the Google Play Store
- A FICS account, or none at all — you can play as a guest. Registration is free
  at [freechess.org](https://www.freechess.org)

Thanks for helping.
