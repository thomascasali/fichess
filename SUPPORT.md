# Support

Fichess is made by one person in his spare time. Clear reports get fixed faster —
here is how to help.

## Before anything else

> **Never share your FICS password.** Not in an issue, not by email, not with
> anyone claiming to be an administrator. Nobody involved with this app will ever
> ask you for it. If you have already posted it somewhere, change it immediately
> with the `password` command on FICS.

## Is it the app, or is it FICS?

Fichess is a client. The games, ratings, accounts and chat all live on the
**Free Internet Chess Server**, which is run by its own volunteer administrators.

**Contact the FICS administrators** at [freechess.org](https://www.freechess.org)
for anything about your account itself:

- registering, or a lost or forgotten password
- your rating, or a game you believe was scored wrongly
- an opponent's behaviour, abuse in chat, bans and censoring
- deleting your FICS account (the app can send the request for you, but the
  administrators are the ones who carry it out)

**Report it here** if it is about the app on your phone:

- the app crashes, freezes or drains the battery
- a move, clock or board is displayed incorrectly
- something in the interface is broken, unreadable or mistranslated
- the app fails to connect, or keeps disconnecting, when the website works fine
- a feature you would like to see

## Reporting a bug

Use **[the bug report form](https://github.com/thomascasali/fichess/issues/new/choose)**.
It asks for what is needed; the more of it you fill in, the better.

The details that make the difference:

**The app version.** Open **Info & Support** — the heart icon at the top left of
the lobby — and read the version number at the very bottom of the screen.

**Your device and OS version.** "iPhone 14, iOS 26.2" or "Pixel 7, Android 16".
Some bugs only appear on certain screen sizes.

**What you did, step by step.** "It broke" is hard to act on. "I created a 3+0
rated game, my opponent accepted, and the clock kept running after my move" is
something that can be reproduced.

**When it happened.** If you can, the approximate time and your FICS username, so
the game can be looked up. Please don't include your password.

**A screenshot or screen recording**, if the problem is visual. On both platforms
you can record the screen and drag the video straight into the issue.

## Reporting objectionable messages or abusive players

The app carries chat written by other people on FICS. If someone sends you
something offensive:

**Report the message.** Press and hold it and choose *Report message*. It reaches
me directly. Reports are reviewed **within 24 hours**: upheld reports get the
content removed from the app, the player blocked, and the account referred to the
FICS administrators for sanction.

**Block the player.** Press and hold one of their messages and choose *Block
player*. Their messages vanish from the app immediately, and the block is applied
on the FICS server too, so they cannot reach you there either. Manage or undo it
from **Settings → Blocked players**.

Offensive language is also filtered automatically: caught messages are hidden
behind a warning you can choose to reveal.

Accounts that repeatedly send objectionable content lose access to the messaging
features of the app. The full policy is in the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).

## Requesting a feature

Use **[the feature request form](https://github.com/thomascasali/fichess/issues/new/choose)**.
Explaining *why* you want something — the situation where the current app gets in
your way — is usually more useful than describing the solution you have in mind.

## What to expect

This is not a company and there is no support desk. Issues are read, but a reply
may take a while, and not every request can be built. Bugs that break play come
first.
